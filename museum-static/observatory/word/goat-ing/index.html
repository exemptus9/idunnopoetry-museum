Possible formation
Capitalized GOAT with -ing converts the greatest-of-all-time acronym into a verb-like form while retaining the animal-word pun.
Possible reading
The recovered sense concerns performing or creating at an exceptional level. The dual reading can let praise and goat imagery coexist in a poem.
Question for the author
Should the verb be intransitive, as in someone GOAT-ing, or can it also take an object such as a performance?
