THE MANIFESTO OF ALMOST

A margin
is a workshop.

The Unfinished is a place for thoughts that haven't decided what to become. A seed isn't a failed tree. A fragment isn't a broken poem. Sometimes the most honest work is still open.

01 /

Explore

Browse fictional writing examples and follow their connections. No algorithmic theatre; just a little curiosity.

02 /

Experiment

Fork a specimen into your own private working copy. Track revisions rather than sacrificing originals.

03 /

Preserve

Export a backup you control. The demo has no cloud storage, user accounts, or automatic publication.

What this public demo isn't

It does not contain Brandon WordSmith's unpublished corpus, private messages, or archived personal notes. Its samples were fictionalized solely for testing the interface. An imported file remains on your own device in your browser's local database, but changes to the site, browser-data deletion, and device failure can still destroy that copy. Keep a separate backup.

Need a next step? Press Ctrl/⌘ + K to search actions by intent. Advanced writing instruments stay collapsed until you need them.
