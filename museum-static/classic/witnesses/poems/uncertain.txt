<br><h1>Uncertain</h1><br>


Day after day I try,<br>
To be as nice as I can,<br>
To hold back the tears as I cry,<br>
And show that I'm a man.<br><br>

I want to be your friend,<br>
To be there for you always,<br>
To have it never end,<br>
To last for the rest of the days.<br><br>

But nothing seems to work,<br>
All you do is reject me,<br>
Am I being a jerk?<br>
Or are you playing "hard to get me?"<br><br>

You hide behind your laughter,<br>
But tears fall at night in your bed,<br>
What do you think I'm after?<br>
I wish I knew what went on in your head...<br><br>

All I want is to help you,<br>
To protect you and to defend,<br>
But in order for me to love you,<br>
You have to let me in.<br><br>

Actions speak louder than words,<br>
And your actions say you hate me,<br>
I know that it seems absurd,<br>
But I've liked you more lately.<br><br><br>
