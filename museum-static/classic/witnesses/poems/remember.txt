<br><h1>I Remember</h1><br>


When I think of you,<br>
In my heart I feel pain,<br>
For then I realize,<br>
I won't see you again.<br><br>

Fondly I think back,<br>
To childhood memories,<br>
When you were much younger,<br>
And had more energy.<br><br>

Back when you could cook a meal,<br>
And could get up and walk,<br>
When you could sit there in your chair all day,<br>
And do nothing but talk.<br><br>

I remember road trips,<br>
Driving for many miles,<br>
Riding in the car for hours,<br>
Just to see your smile.<br><br>

I remember being upset,<br>
When I found out you would die,<br>
And I'd give anything,<br>
If I could just have said goodbye.<br><br>

I want you to know that I love you,<br>
And I want you to know this too:<br>
I will never forget you,<br>
You're special, and I miss you.<br><br><br>
