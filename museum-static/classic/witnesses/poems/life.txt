<br><h1>Life</h1><br>


In life I've found out lots,<br>
And as near as I can tell,<br>
You have to take the shots,<br>
And things aren't always well.<br><br>

No matter where you turn,<br>
Some things are hard to see,<br>
One thing that I've learned,<br>
Hatred only brings misery.<br><br>

What's the point of trying?<br>
When you've got nothing to gain,<br>
I know as I sit here crying,<br>
Only people who love feel pain.<br><br>

When everyone around,<br>
Tells you what you do,<br>
Sometimes you wonder aloud,<br>
Maybe what they say is true.<br><br>

Seems the worst pain's left unspoken,<br>
Silently coming up behind,<br>
When all love is made to be broken,<br>
And nothing's what it is in the light.<br><br><br>
