<br><h1>Shallow</h1><br>

<pre>
Shallow is shallow
Can't see beyond the surface
Can't go a little deeper
Can't try a little harder

Shallow's as shallow as its shadow
As shallow as the half empty glass
Except... more shallow...

For Shallow is callow,
Immature in its perception
Immature in its view,
Immature, Shallow's you.
</pre>
