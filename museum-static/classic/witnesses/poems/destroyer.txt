<br><h1>The Destroyer</h1><br>


Suffocate me with words of hate,<br>
Drown me in feelings of scorn,<br>
Choke me with your evil thoughts,<br>
Smother me 'till I can take no more.<br><br>

Asphyxiate my happiness,<br>
Take away its breath,<br>
Pound the nails into its skin,<br>
Beat it near to death.<br><br>

Remove the blindfold from my eyes,<br>
Tell me what I see,<br>
Erase the joy, erase the love,<br>
I followed so carelessly.<br><br>

Tie the rope around my neck,<br>
And watch me take the fall,<br>
Poison me with bitter lies,<br>
Ignore my heart's loudest call.<br><br>

Burn the structure of my love,<br>
And set my life on fire,<br>
Destroy everything like you said you wouldn't,<br>
And take my life, you liar.<br><br><br>
