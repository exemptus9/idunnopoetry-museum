<br><h1>I Know</h1><br>

<pre>
I know how you feel,
I've been there so many times,
I know what it's like to hate yourself,
And do nothing all day but cry.

I know how bad it hurts,
When someone plays with your heart,
But just like the Fragile says,
I won't let you fall apart.

I know that things are rough,
But remember, I'm here for you,
Things WILL get better,
Trust me, they always do.

I know how life can beat you down,
But you have to get back up,
If you don't, you'll never win,
And wouldn't that just suck?

I know that you feel worthless,
But you know that can't be true,
If it were I wouldn't love you,
But luckily I do.
</pre>
