<br><h1>The Reason</h1><br>


All my life I have searched,<br>
For one person to truly care,<br>
Someone I can always talk to,<br>
Someone who is always there.<br><br>

Someone who won't let me down,<br>
And if they do will bring me back up,<br>
One who makes me happy inside,<br>
At the slightest sensation of touch.<br><br>

I've looked and tried with many,<br>
But all I could find was hurt,<br>
And now I want you to know,<br>
I want us to please make this work.<br><br>

I've wondered if life was worth living,<br>
And now I can say that's true,<br>
For all of the things that you've done,<br>
I want you to know I love you.<br><br><br>
