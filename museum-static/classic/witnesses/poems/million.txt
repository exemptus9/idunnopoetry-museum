<br><h1>Million</h1><br>

<pre>
A million different things
Spinning through my mind
A million thoughts I wish to put into words
But can't

Even if I could
I could not tell them to her
I am silenced
And restrained

A million curses
For a million broken promises
A million violent fantasies
Crossing my dreams each night

A million ways to kill
A million ways it'll never work out
A million ways to die
A million voices laughing

Laughing at her pathetic attempts
Laughing as she cries
Laughing as she loses her most valuable possession

One million words of hate piercing like daggers
One million lies breaking my heart
Into one million jagged edges

One million kicks to her self esteem
As I laugh
One million daggers in her heart
As she loses all as I did.
</pre>
