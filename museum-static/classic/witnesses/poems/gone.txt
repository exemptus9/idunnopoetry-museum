<br><h1>Gone</h1><br>


My love for you burns like a fire,<br>
I need the heat and I want to be near,<br>
When I'm with you I feel ten times higher,<br>
But things have changed I fear.<br><br>

Gone are the days I longed to be,<br>
Within your marvelous presence,<br>
Scrambling to try so earnestly,<br>
To see you again every chance.<br><br>

I now am filled with deep remorse,<br>
For my love was taken in vain,<br>
Like racing through the driving course,<br>
Learning you're in the wrong lane.<br><br>

For so long you were the object of my affection,<br>
The one I thought of daily and nightly too,<br>
I realized what was wrong and made the correction,<br>
Finally realized that I'm not meant for you.<br><br>

You lie and steal from poor fools like me,<br>
Stupid enough to foolishly give their love away,<br>
I want you to know I will no longer be,<br>
Stupid enough to love you one more day.<br><br><br>
