<br><h1>Dive</h1><br>

<pre>
Reflection in the water,
The face inside that's me,
Ripples break the surface,
Like waves among the sea.

Rain falls down in torrents,
Lightning lights the sky,
Dreams may fall and crumble,
But wishes never die.

Stepping off, descent,
Ends within a splash,
Swallowed by the depths,
Of the foamy wash.

Underwater shelter,
Save me from the world,
Floating in the liquid,
All the while curled.

Ocean pulls me close,
The warmth, it slowly grows,
The Ocean understands me,
It's the only one that knows.
</pre>
