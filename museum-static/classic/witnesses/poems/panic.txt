<br><h1>Panic</h1><br>


At night when I'm alone,<br>
And lying in my bed,<br>
Things talk to me,<br>
They live inside my head.<br><br>

I don't know what they say,<br>
But I'm overcome with fear,<br>
I keep trying to ignore it,<br>
Hoping it will disappear.<br><br>

I sit there and I pray,<br>
For God to bring me peace,<br>
To take away the fear,<br>
And to please just let me sleep.<br><br>

So I lay there still,<br>
Waiting for sleep to come,<br>
Praying for tomorrow,<br>
For this night to be done.<br><br><br>
