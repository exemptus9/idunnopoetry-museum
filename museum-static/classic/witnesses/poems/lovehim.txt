<br><h1>You Love Him</h1><br>


I see from across the room,<br>
In his direction you stare,<br>
Silently I wonder,<br>
Why it's him for whom you care.<br><br>

Your every breath, your every thought,<br>
Consumed with visions of him,<br>
Every course of action, every move you make,<br>
Based on lusts and whims.<br><br>

It seems like to me,<br>
You want what you can't have,<br>
Take for granted what you do,<br>
And just shake it off and laugh.<br><br>

If you neglect your true friends,<br>
On the gamble of just one person,<br>
You'll have no one there to help,<br>
And your problems will only worsen.<br><br>

But liking him is your choice,<br>
I cannot change your mind,<br>
I am but just one small voice,<br>
Who is trying to be kind.<br><br>

So with tears streaming down my face,<br>
I will let you leave,<br>
Just remember when everyone's gone,<br>
You'll still always have me.<br><br><br>
