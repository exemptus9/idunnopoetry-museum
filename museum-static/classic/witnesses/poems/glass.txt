<br><h1>Glass Onion</h1><br>

<pre>
I speak my words through onion breath 
There's more to what I say 
To see beyond the surface 
You must peel the layers away 

On the outside it looks ugly 
But dig through deeper down 
Have an open mind 
Persevere till beauty's found 

I cannot find the value in 
Finding something ugly 
It seems more easy in my mind 
To find everything lovely 

Instead of traveling through life 
With views so dark and hateful 
For every moment we are here 
We should be ever greatful 

And though we walk through fiery trials 
Instead of merely screaming 
We should find the good and light in pain 
Embrace and cling to meaning
</pre>
