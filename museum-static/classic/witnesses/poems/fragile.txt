<br><h1>Fragile</h1><br>


Have you ever held a glass,<br>
And felt its fragility?<br>
Or watched a deer running,<br>
And marveled at it's agility?<br><br>

Or looked at the world around,<br>
And seen how quickly it would fall,<br>
If the thing that held it up was gone,<br>
And then you lost it all?<br><br>

My life is like the glass,<br>
It's fragile, and does not bend,<br>
With the only constant in relationships,<br>
Is that eventually they'll end.<br><br>

You are like the table,<br>
Supporting and standing tall,<br>
Keeping me from plunging down,<br>
Holding me up, preventing the fall.<br><br>

The Table is taken from the glass,<br>
The ground rushing quickly near,<br>
Falling quickly and silently,<br>
Like the agile deer.<br><br>

No way to escape gravity,<br>
With a smash, pieces fly through the room,<br>
The sound of my world crashing down,<br>
Knowing my dreams will be gone soon.<br><br>

So as I lay in pieces,<br>
I pray I get put back together,<br>
That someone will come be my glue,<br>
And keep me together forever.<br><br><br>
