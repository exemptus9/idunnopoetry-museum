<br><h1>The Sardonic</h1><br>

<pre>
Come with us
Run with us
And all the fires of Hell
Dumb like us
Succumb like us
Before the timely knell
Used like us
Abused like us
We made your problems swell
Mused like us
Confused like us
But no one else can tell
Try like us
And die like us
Within your final yell
Cry like us
Imply like us
That you already fell
</pre>
