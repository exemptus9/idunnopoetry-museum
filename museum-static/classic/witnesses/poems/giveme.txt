<br><h1>Give Me</h1><br>

<pre>
Give me your hate,
Give me your fear,
Give me your worries,
And they'll disappear.

Give me your sadness,
Give me your pain,
Give me your storms,
And I'll stop the rain.

Give me the hurt,
Give me the shit,
Give me it all,
I'll get rid of it.

Give me the pieces,
Of your broken heart,
Give me your world,
As it falls apart.

Give me your problems,
I'll bear them for you,
Give me your life,
When there's nothing to lose.

Give me your love,
And you'll have mine forever,
No matter the problem,
We'll fix it together.
</pre>
