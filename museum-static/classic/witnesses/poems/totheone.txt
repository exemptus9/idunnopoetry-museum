<br><h1>To the One I Love</h1><br>


To the one I love,<br> 
The one that I adore,<br>
My only goal in life, <br>
The one that I live for:<br><br>

Even though we fight,<br>
More than we get along,<br>
I hope our relationship,<br>
Will never go wrong.<br><br>

I love to be with you, <br>
I love when you're around; <br>
I'm so glad I'm the one,<br>
That you just now found.<br><br>

Though you may not see,<br>
Just what you mean to me,<br>
My love for you forever,<br>
And ever will always be.<br><br>

I know you love me,<br>
And I know that you care;<br>
That is why I treasure,<br>
The love that we share.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
That I am here for you,<br>
Whatever is going on,<br>
I will help you through.<br><br>

To the one I love, <br>
The one that I adore:<br>
With each passing day,<br>
I love you more and more.<br><br><br>
