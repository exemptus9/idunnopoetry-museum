<br><h1>The Descent of Happiness</h1><br>


There! I can see it!<br>
Love within my grasp!<br>
I reach for it, it slips away,<br>
I knew it wouldn't last.<br><br>

Now life is slipping away,<br>
Smashed up all my dreams,<br>
Breaking down and broken,<br>
Dying more each day it seems.<br><br>

I was at first so happy,<br>
Now everything has fallen apart,<br>
Tearing open my insides,<br>
And ripping out my heart.<br><br>

In this flailing madness,<br>
What I once loved now despised,<br>
Cowering at the sight of you,<br>
Covering my eyes.<br><br>

In this bitter victory,<br>
I feel like love I have won,<br>
And as I look back at the battle,<br>
I see my goal, my love, is gone.<br><br><br>
