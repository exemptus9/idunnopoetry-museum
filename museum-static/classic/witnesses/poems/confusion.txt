<br><h1>Confusion</h1><br>


I've been searching,<br>
For someone lovely,<br>
All I want,<br>
Is someone who loves me.<br><br>

Someone who cares,<br>
If I'm happy or not,<br>
And when I screw up,<br>
Whose love will not stop.<br><br>

You make it all better,<br>
All bad things go away,<br>
You help me so much,<br>
And give strength for another day.<br><br>

I love you so much,<br>
I hope we make it through,<br>
You can make me smile,<br>
Something few people can do.<br><br>

You mend all that's broken,<br>
Pull together the pieces,<br>
You give me reason to live,<br>
And the strength to release it.<br><br>

I want to make you happy,<br>
Somehow I wish to repay,<br>
I want to be with you forever,<br>
And try the rest of my days.<br><br>

I know I really love you,<br>
But you're too good to be true,<br>
And I know that it sounds crazy,<br>
But I'm afraid of losing you.<br><br>

I love you, but...<br>
I feel I love too much,<br>
I know you love me, but...<br>
It doesn't seem like it's enough.<br><br>

I think I love you too much,<br>
Even though it's what you deserve,<br>
But loving you too much,<br>
I'm sure, just gets on your nerves.<br><br>

I don't like one-sided love,<br>
In a way it makes me feel used,<br>
Understand that I'm not mad,<br>
I'm just a bit confused.<br><br>

Of course I love you more,<br>
But I need to be loved back,<br>
It sounds like you don't love me,<br>
But I know you can't mean that.<br><br>

So I will come down to your level,<br>
And make both our loves equal,<br>
So that they may grow together,<br>
And in that be more meaningful.<br><br>

You yourself are the one,<br>
I'm always thinking of,<br>
But I'm still afraid of losing,<br>
The only one I love.<br><br><br>
