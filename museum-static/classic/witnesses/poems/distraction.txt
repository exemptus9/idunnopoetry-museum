<br><h1>Distraction</h1><br>


The days we were together,<br>
Were the best I ever knew,<br>
And if you weren't there with me,<br>
I'm not sure what I'd do.<br><br>

Each day was a little better,<br>
So fun it can't be explained,<br>
In the aftermath of your presence,<br>
I'm left wanting to see you again.<br><br>

But even though the world,<br>
Was slowly crashing down around me,<br>
You were by my side,<br>
And helped forgetfulness surround me.<br><br>

You live too far away,<br>
For us to go out or date,<br>
And even if you lived next door,<br>
We still could not relate.<br><br>

Even though I love so much,<br>
I can never have you,<br>
Circumstances beyond control,<br>
There's nothing I can do.<br><br>

Though we cannot be together,<br>
Still, at least I wish,<br>
I could see you more often,<br>
Because you're so fun to be with.<br><br><br>
