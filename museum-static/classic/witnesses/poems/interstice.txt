<br><h1>Interstice</h1><br>

<pre>
On lonely nights I sit,
And trace my thoughts across the sky,
And watch them form the visions,
Of the lives of you and I.

I dream of days to come,
When the distance disappears,
And thank the Lord in Heaven,
For each second that it nears.

I know it in my heart,
That you're the one He's sent,
I'll know I lived a good life,
If with you it's spent.

So I close my eyes
As I invite my slumber over
And contentedly I sigh,
As I realize we're one day closer.
</pre>
