<br><h1>Poem</h1><br>

<pre>
This my friends, it is a poem 
And if you need I can go slow 
I know how sleepy you all are 
Cause I am too, all day so far 
So sing a song and dance and laugh 
Enjoy your hobbies and your crafts 
But as for me and my sweet home 
We'll rock this party all night long
</pre>
