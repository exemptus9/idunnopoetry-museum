<br><h1>Time</h1><br>

<pre>
Days slipping away,
Just out of our grasp,
There goes another day,
Gone into the past.

It doesn’t seem to last,
At all very long,
So while these moments pass,
Let’s try to hold on.

The minutes slip by us,
Faster and faster still,
The seconds they deny us,
Are against our will.

So lets hold on to these moments,
Forever as long as can be,
Now put down your defense,
And let yourself be free.

Let’s make these good times last,
Be together, happy at least,
Before they’re gone into the past,
Just out of our reach.
</pre>
