<br><h1>Bad Thoughts</h1><br>


I took the knife at night,<br>
And pressed the blade against my skin,<br>
And thought if only I could die,<br>
Maybe then at least I'd win.<br><br>

And if I won, what would I gain?<br>
Release from temporary pain,<br>
Until the numbness fades away,<br>
And pain's left to take its place.<br><br><br><br>
