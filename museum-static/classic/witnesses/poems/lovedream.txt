<br><h1>The Love Dream</h1><br>


In a dream, I am our love,<br>
Dangling from the edge of repair,<br>
High, high above the ground,<br>
Above the pit of despair.<br><br>

Clawing and grabbing,<br>
I'm losing my grip,<br>
My fingers start aching,<br>
They're starting to slip.<br><br>

I look down below,<br>
Gauging the distance,<br>
In my mind I know,<br>
That I must resist it.<br><br>

But you can't resist it,<br>
After all, it's gravity,<br>
Nothing I can do to save it,<br>
From this tragedy.<br><br>

It really gets me down,<br>
To know that there's no way,<br>
So I let go of the ledge,<br>
And just accept my fate..<br><br><br>
