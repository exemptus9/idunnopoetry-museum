<br><h1>I Want You to Know</h1><br>


I want you to know,<br>
Just a few things,<br>
The hate that you've caused,<br>
And the sadness it brings.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
What you've done to me,<br>
How it tears me apart,<br>
And makes me lonely.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
How sad that I've been,<br>
How hopeless you seem,<br>
And how you won't let me in.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
How difficult you are,<br>
Every time that I leave,<br>
I don't get very far.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
There's something about you,<br>
Something that tells me,<br>
I can't live without you.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
How much I want to help,<br>
How everything's for you,<br>
And nothing's for myself.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
I think of you daily,<br>
Though you tick me off,<br>
I love you anyway.<br><br>

I want you to know,<br>
That I'm completely sincere,<br>
No matter what it is,<br>
I will always be here.<br><br><br>
