<br><h1>Sorry</h1><br>

<pre>
I'm sorry that I get upset,
Whenever you do wrong,
And I'm sorry that our time together,
Never lasts that long.

I'm sorry that I love you,
So much that when you fail,
To spend your time with me,
I make your life a living hell.

I'm sorry I enjoy,
The pleasure your body provides,
And if I said I didn't like it,
Then I'd be telling a lie.

I'm sorry that I love you so,
That I can't live without you,
I'm sorry that I care so much,
I always think about you.

I'm sorry I tell you I love you,
So much it's like the song from Barney,
But the scary thing to me is,
True love's not having to say you're sorry.
