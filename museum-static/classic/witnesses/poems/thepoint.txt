<br><h1>What's the Point?</h1><br>


What's the point of trying,<br>
When all you do is wrong?<br>
What's the point of crying,<br>
When no one hears your song?<br><br>

What's the point of love,<br>
When nothing goes your way?<br>
What's the point of effort,<br>
When you can't take another day?<br><br>

What's the point of memories?<br>
They all will fade away,<br>
What's the point of sensitivity?<br>
You'll just get called gay...<br><br>

What's the point of happiness?<br>
It lasts only a short time,<br>
What's the point of sadness?<br>
It just ruins your life.<br><br>

What's the point of seeing?<br>
When everything's a lie?<br>
What's the point of believing?<br>
When all you ask is why?<br><br>

What's the point of life?<br>
Filled with pain and strife,<br>
And what's the point of me?<br>
Filled with sadness and misery.<br><br><br>
