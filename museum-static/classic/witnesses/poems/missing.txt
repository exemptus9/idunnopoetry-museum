<br><h1>Missing You</h1><br>


When we are apart,<br>
My heart is torn open,<br>
But right from the start,<br>
You left my heart broken.<br><br>

Somehow I still love you,<br>
As I always will,<br>
But words just won't do,<br>
You need to see that it's real.<br><br>

I wish somehow I could convey,<br>
How you make me feel,<br>
How I think of you every day,<br>
And how I love you still.<br><br>

It's very hard at most,<br>
When I don't know what you think,<br>
Just when I feel we're getting close,<br>
I screw up and your feelings sink.<br><br>

I say in advance,<br>
That I'm sorry for what I'll do,<br>
So I take you by the hands,<br>
And ask, "Do you love me too?"<br><br><br>
