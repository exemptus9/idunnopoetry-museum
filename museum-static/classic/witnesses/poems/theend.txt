<br><h1>The End</h1><br>


I soar on wings, of broken dreams,<br>
And heed the facts as mere warnings,<br>
And hurt the ones who love me most,<br>
Then wonder why I can't get close.<br><br>

I see the beauty in your eyes,<br>
And silently I wonder why,<br>
We can't get closer than we are,<br>
Why gradually we drift apart.<br><br>

I loved you more than words could say,<br>
And could not go a single day,<br>
Without the beauty of your voice,<br>
Echoing with words of choice.<br><br>

I longed to have you here with me,<br>
To have you near, so you could see,<br>
How much I loved, how much I cared,<br>
How love that deep was truly rare.<br><br>

How much I longed for happiness,<br>
Compared to mine, yours was less,<br>
And even though I was worthless,<br>
My love for you I did confess.<br><br>

The world around, comes crashing down,<br>
I cannot reach; I'm tied and bound,<br>
And if you loved me, you would care,<br>
And rescue me from this despair,<br><br>

And save me from my mind's worst fears,<br>
And dam the river of my tears,<br>
And make some effort just to help,<br>
Before I snap and hurt myself.<br><br>

Before I snap, and hurt the ones,<br>
Who loved me first, and not for fun,<br>
Who love me when, I'm sick and ill,<br>
And though I hurt them, love me still.<br><br>

And though I always screw things up,<br>
They'll always come to lift me up,<br>
They'll always love me, and they'll care,<br>
When life is rough and is not fair.<br><br>

When girls like you, take my heart,<br>
And rip and smash and tear it apart,<br>
And walk me through, a living hell,<br>
Where nothing's good, and nothing's well.<br><br>

Where I remind, myself how bad,<br>
That things can get, when I am sad,<br>
I always seem, to end up hurt,<br>
And then somehow, end up a jerk.<br><br>

I don't see how, that could be true,<br>
When all I wanted, was for you,<br>
To tell me that, you loved me too,<br>
So I know what I must do.<br><br>

I am breaking up with you,<br>
And breaking my whole life in two,<br>
And even though I don't want to,<br>
It is something that I must do.<br><br>

Don't come around, and please don't call,<br>
Don't even bother me at all,<br>
Don't tell me that you're sad today,<br>
You were going to anyway.<br><br><br>
