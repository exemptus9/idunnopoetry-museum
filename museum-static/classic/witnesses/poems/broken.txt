<br><h1>Broken</h1><br>


You look at this world with hatred,<br>
Anger burns in your heart,<br>
All the things that have been said,<br>
Are tearing your life apart.<br><br>

Sadness overwhelms you,<br>
You cry all day and night,<br>
The stuff you try to do,<br>
Makes you wonder: is life worth the fight?<br><br>

I come to you and write this,<br>
In hopes that you will see,<br>
That you can't just be faithless,<br>
That life is what you make it to be.<br><br>

I know that things seem bad,<br>
But you WILL get through the days,<br>
They're exactly like a fad:<br>
They're bound to go away.<br><br>

On days you feel you can't go on,<br>
And you've done all you think you can do,<br>
Please just remember this poem,<br>
And know I'll be there for you.<br><br><br>
