<br><h1>Dream</h1><br>


Laying on the beach,<br>
On a cool summer's night,<br>
You within my reach,<br>
Bathed in pale moonlight.<br><br>

All at once we're taken,<br>
Lifted high above the clouds,<br>
I look and try to make them,<br>
Take us to the ground.<br><br>

Far below I can see,<br>
Lights within the waves,<br>
And the person holding me,<br>
Says, "We couldn't be saved."<br><br>

Below in the water floating,<br>
Two objects drifting a sea,<br>
And I realize with heart beating,<br>
The objects are you and me.<br><br>

Looking up I spied,<br>
The gates of heaven's door,<br>
And realized that we died,<br>
To live in heaven forevermore.<br><br>
