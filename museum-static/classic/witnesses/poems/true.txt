<br><h1>True Perfection</h1><br>

<pre>
Last night I experienced perfection,
Everything was perfect between us,
The only way it could be better,
Would be if she could have seen us.

I was happy, you were happy,
We loved each other so much,
I fought to stay forever,
To live my dreams, but had no luck.

It was the perfect world,
The perfect place, the perfect scene,
The place that was so perfect,
I couldn't believe it wasn't a dream.

And even though I can't go back,
At least it was escape,
At least for once forgot the past,
At least forgot my hate.

But things are getting better,
I love this twist of fate,
It's almost like a dream,
From which I pray I never wake.
</pre>
