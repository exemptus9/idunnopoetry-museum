<br><h1>Deception</h1><br>


The lies flow like water,<br>
The hate pours out like rain,<br>
Fear and confusion settle in,<br>
Joy's been all but slain.<br><br>

Lured me into your web,<br>
Baited me into your trap,<br>
Disoriented and confused,<br>
Like being lost without a map.<br><br>

I see you walking by,<br>
Your face that I despise,<br>
I'd rather be beaten with the truth,<br>
Than tortured by your lies.<br><br>

The things you couldn't tell me,<br>
Were the things I always knew,<br>
And even though you said they weren't,<br>
I knew that they were true.<br><br>

Now it's in the past,<br>
And you probably forgot,<br>
But I will never let it go,<br>
I've come too far to stop.<br><br>

One day you will pay,<br>
For all the pain that you've caused,<br>
Right now you may think that you've won,<br>
But you haven't; you have lost.<br><br><br>
