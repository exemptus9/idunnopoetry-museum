<br><h1>Scars</h1><br>


Skin is thin and unbroken,<br>
Like words of hate, left unspoken,<br>
Until the blade of conflict cuts,<br>
The skin that spreads, as opened up.<br><br>

The blood flows out like crimson streams,<br>
Surrounds and drowns the strongest screams,<br>
Until the river Red runs dry,<br>
All else is left alone to die.<br><br>

The wound, it slowly closes up,<br>
And mends until it closes shut,<br>
It hides the pain it felt before,<br>
And waits for pain that's left in store.<br><br>

Scars appear where wounds once healed,<br>
And still reveal where holes were sealed,<br>
Remind of times of massive loss,<br>
When losing canceled out the cost.<br><br>

The only thing prolonging pain,<br>
Are memories inside the brain,<br>
That if erased, would put to rest,<br>
Reminders of the pain that's left.<br><br><br>
