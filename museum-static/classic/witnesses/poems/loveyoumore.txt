<br><h1>I Love You More</h1><br>


I love you more than life itself,<br>
For life in itself is worthless,<br>
Without someone with which to share,<br>
The beauty of life's purpose.<br><br>

I love you more than you can know,<br>
You've helped more than you can imagine,<br>
I once was lonely, now I see,<br>
And realize miracles still happen.<br><br>

I love you more for who you are,<br>
Than what you can or will be,<br>
Whenever I am feeling empty,<br>
You come along and fill me.<br><br>

I love you more than happiness,<br>
For my happiness comes from you,<br>
For so long I waited for someone,<br>
And now my wish has come true.<br><br>

I love you more with each passing day,<br>
More than anyone before,<br>
But no matter how much you love me,<br>
I will always love you more.<br><br><br>
