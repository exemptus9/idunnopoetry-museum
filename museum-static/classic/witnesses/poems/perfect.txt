<br><h1>Perfect</h1><br>


Last night, I dreamt of perfection,<br>
Everything was perfect between us,<br>
We were how we used to be,<br>
It was perfect, you should have seen us.<br><br>

I was happy, you were happy,<br>
We loved each other so much,<br>
I fought to stay asleep,<br>
To live the dream, but had no luck.<br><br>

I dreamed of the perfect world,<br>
The perfect place, the perfect scene,<br>
The place that I would stay forever,<br>
If the place was not a dream.<br><br>

And even though it is not real,<br>
At least I could escape,<br>
At least I could forget the past,<br>
At least forget my hate.<br><br>

My mind is filled with memories,<br>
Which are all that I have left,<br>
And I'd use the keys of happiness,<br>
If I knew where they were kept.<br><br>

But things are never well for me,<br>
I guess it's just my fate,<br>
It's dreams like this, that make me wish,<br>
I'd sleep and never wake.<br><br>
