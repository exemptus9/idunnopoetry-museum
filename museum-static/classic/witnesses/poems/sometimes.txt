<br><h1>Sometimes</h1><br>


Sometimes I feel as if I'm falling,<br>
And can never come back again,<br>
And that no matter how hard I'm trying,<br>
I'll never be able to win.<br><br>

I feel as if there's nothing left,<br>
Every corner now explored,<br>
There's nothing to look forward to,<br>
Nothing good is left in store.<br><br>

Sometimes I wish that I could be,<br>
A better person than I am,<br>
That I could be a better Christian,<br>
That I could be a better friend.<br><br>

Sometimes I am so happy,<br>
That I don't think I can stand it,<br>
And other times I get so mad,<br>
It feels like someone planned it.<br><br>

Sometimes I drift and wander,<br>
And wonder why that I'm alive,<br>
What I'm supposed to do,<br>
And wonder what's the point of life.<br><br>

Sometimes I feel worthless,<br>
But I know it can't be true,<br>
If I was you wouldn't love me,<br>
But luckily you do.<br><br><br>
