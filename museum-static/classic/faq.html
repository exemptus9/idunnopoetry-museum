Frequently Asked Questions

Selected historical excerpts. These responses derive from the original faq.shtml. Outdated personal details and obsolete contact information are omitted. They describe the author's views at the time, not necessarily now.
What is your mission with the site?

My mission is multi-faceted. I seek firstly to entertain, and to move emotionally. I seek also to describe as best possible situations and events and their emotional counterparts. This site is also a form of therapy, not only for myself, but for all those who wish to read it and experience it with me.

What is (name of poem) about?

Well most of my poems are fairly self-explanatory, but I realize some of them can be a little vague. Most of them are about past relationships, but I like to keep things somewhat vague so that they are open to interpretation and easier for people to relate to.

What do you write about?

Things I go through and experience mostly, as well as my feelings, observations, things I feel strongly about, life, and occasionally fiction.

Why does most of your poetry rhyme?

Because in my opinion, it takes less skill to write stuff that doesn't rhyme. Anyone can just write down anything, but it takes a lot more work to have the poem make sense AND rhyme.

How often do you write?

Whenever I am able to. Firstly, I have to be "inspired" and second, my writing ability has to be with me.

How did you make this site?

I designed the layout and art myself. I created all graphics on this site using Paint Shop Pro and Photoshop. I wrote the "code" in Notepad, and the site is written in HTML using a dash of CSS for the styling, some Javascript here and there, with PHP handling the more complex tasks with MySQL taking care of the database work.
