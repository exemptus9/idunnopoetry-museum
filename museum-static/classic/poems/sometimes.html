Sometimes

View all poems
Sometimes I feel as if I'm falling,
And can never come back again,
And that no matter how hard I'm trying,
I'll never be able to win.

I feel as if there's nothing left,
Every corner now explored,
There's nothing to look forward to,
Nothing good is left in store.

Sometimes I wish that I could be,
A better person than I am,
That I could be a better Christian,
That I could be a better friend.

Sometimes I am so happy,
That I don't think I can stand it,
And other times I get so mad,
It feels like someone planned it.

Sometimes I drift and wander,
And wonder why that I'm alive,
What I'm supposed to do,
And wonder what's the point of life.

Sometimes I feel worthless,
But I know it can't be true,
If I was you wouldn't love me,
But luckily you do.
