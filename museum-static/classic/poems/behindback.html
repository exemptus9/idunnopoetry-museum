Behind Your Back

View all poems
I wanna scar my face for all to see 
The way I feel's your fault to me 
Why can't you just talk to me? 
'Cause being strong's so hard to be 

Now all I once thought that I knew 
Has turned out fake, havn't you? 
And if you knew, you'd panic too 
Seems I can't have truth and have you too 

I find myself inside your trap 
How could I be so blind as that? 
I shed these tears behind your back 
The good times, can't we rewind to that? 

I guess since time just flows one way 
To try is futile, or so some say 
I'm torn between to go and stay 
And which is right? I'll know one day.
