You Love Him

View all poems
I see from across the room,
In his direction you stare,
Silently I wonder,
Why it's him for whom you care.

Your every breath, your every thought,
Consumed with visions of him,
Every course of action, every move you make,
Based on lusts and whims.

It seems like to me,
You want what you can't have,
Take for granted what you do,
And just shake it off and laugh.

If you neglect your true friends,
On the gamble of just one person,
You'll have no one there to help,
And your problems will only worsen.

But liking him is your choice,
I cannot change your mind,
I am but just one small voice,
Who is trying to be kind.

So with tears streaming down my face,
I will let you leave,
Just remember when everyone's gone,
You'll still always have me.
