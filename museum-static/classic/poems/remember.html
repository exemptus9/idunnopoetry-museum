I Remember

View all poems
When I think of you,
In my heart I feel pain,
For then I realize,
I won't see you again.

Fondly I think back,
To childhood memories,
When you were much younger,
And had more energy.

Back when you could cook a meal,
And could get up and walk,
When you could sit there in your chair all day,
And do nothing but talk.

I remember road trips,
Driving for many miles,
Riding in the car for hours,
Just to see your smile.

I remember being upset,
When I found out you would die,
And I'd give anything,
If I could just have said goodbye.

I want you to know that I love you,
And I want you to know this too:
I will never forget you,
You're special, and I miss you.
