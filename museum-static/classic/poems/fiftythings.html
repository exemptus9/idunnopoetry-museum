Fifty Things I Love About You

View all poems
From your beautiful hair,
To your petite little hands,
To the clothes that you wear,
To the way that you dance,

Your cute little nose,
The way you're so sweet,
The beauty that goes,
From your head to your feet,

The way that you smell,
That's so very lovely,
The way I can tell,
That you really love me,

How you call every day,
The conversations we have,
The funny things you say,
The way you make me laugh,

How you say you'll "get me,"
How your love is so rare,
How you always say you miss me,
And wish that I was there,

The beating of your heart,
As we lay close together,
How I wish we wouldn't part,
And could stay like this forever,

From the things that you're into,
To the way that you're nice,
How I want to be with you,
The rest of my life,

How I never doubt you,
When you make me wait,
The little things about you,
That I love to hate,

Your wonderful smile,
Your beautiful eyes,
How I waited a while,
To see your surprise,

How you love God,
And are not afraid to show it,
How you love me lots,
And how you let me know it,

The way that you're so kind,
Sweet, beautiful, and loving,
How you're always on my mind,
And without you I am nothing,

The way you're always cold,
The way I know you must be,
And when your hand I hold,
How I remember that I'm lucky,

How you love me so,
And never want to leave me,
How you don't want me to go,
And every chance you see me,

How I wish I could be there,
How I think of you every night,
How I would hold you when you're scared,
And tell you everything's all right,

The way there is so much about you,
Too much for me to name,
I don't know what I'd do without you,
I'd never be the same.

These are the things I love,
From your personality to your looks,
And fifty's not enough,
I could write enough to fill fifteen books,

Every reason I listed is true,
I must be your biggest fan,
But the greatest thing about you,
Is how you love me for who I am.
