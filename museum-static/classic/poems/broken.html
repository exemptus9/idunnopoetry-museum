Broken

View all poems
You look at this world with hatred,
Anger burns in your heart,
All the things that have been said,
Are tearing your life apart.

Sadness overwhelms you,
You cry all day and night,
The stuff you try to do,
Makes you wonder: is life worth the fight?

I come to you and write this,
In hopes that you will see,
That you can't just be faithless,
That life is what you make it to be.

I know that things seem bad,
But you WILL get through the days,
They're exactly like a fad:
They're bound to go away.

On days you feel you can't go on,
And you've done all you think you can do,
Please just remember this poem,
And know I'll be there for you.
