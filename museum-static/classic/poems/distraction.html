Distraction

View all poems
The days we were together,
Were the best I ever knew,
And if you weren't there with me,
I'm not sure what I'd do.

Each day was a little better,
So fun it can't be explained,
In the aftermath of your presence,
I'm left wanting to see you again.

But even though the world,
Was slowly crashing down around me,
You were by my side,
And helped forgetfulness surround me.

You live too far away,
For us to go out or date,
And even if you lived next door,
We still could not relate.

Even though I love so much,
I can never have you,
Circumstances beyond control,
There's nothing I can do.

Though we cannot be together,
Still, at least I wish,
I could see you more often,
Because you're so fun to be with.
