I Want You to Know

View all poems
I want you to know,
Just a few things,
The hate that you've caused,
And the sadness it brings.

I want you to know,
What you've done to me,
How it tears me apart,
And makes me lonely.

I want you to know,
How sad that I've been,
How hopeless you seem,
And how you won't let me in.

I want you to know,
How difficult you are,
Every time that I leave,
I don't get very far.

I want you to know,
There's something about you,
Something that tells me,
I can't live without you.

I want you to know,
How much I want to help,
How everything's for you,
And nothing's for myself.

I want you to know,
I think of you daily,
Though you tick me off,
I love you anyway.

I want you to know,
That I'm completely sincere,
No matter what it is,
I will always be here.
