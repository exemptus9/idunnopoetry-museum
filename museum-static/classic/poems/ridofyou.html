Rid of You

View all poems
You know I'd get rid of you if I could.
But I can't seem to get rid of you,
Like you got rid of me.

So I take my hands off your neck,
And stop choking you.
As I let you go,
In my mind,
You die.

I've finally gotten rid of you.
