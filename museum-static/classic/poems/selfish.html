Selfish?

View all poems
I look and all I f-cking see
Are memories of you and me
Reminding of pain carved into
A fading memory of you

What you don't want, you cannot miss
The damage done cannot be fixed
Much less by a five lettered word
Which in this case should be a verb

However it's simply a lie
A noun expressed within a sigh
If you were sorry, you would help
Someone other than yourself

And selfish though to some I sound
I'd like to now turn that around
For which of us neglected feelings?
Promises, and other dealings?

Who here broke the other's heart?
And who left who to fall apart?
Who betrayed their "bestest friend?"
And brought their life near to an end?

Her you see, I broke no trust
All I wanted, was her love
But such a simple thing as this
Was given to a stupid prick

But after all, it's just a girl
There's many of them in this world
There's always more fish in the sea
But it doesn't matter, when none are for me...
