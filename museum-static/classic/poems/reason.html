The Reason

View all poems
All my life I have searched,
For one person to truly care,
Someone I can always talk to,
Someone who is always there.

Someone who won't let me down,
And if they do will bring me back up,
One who makes me happy inside,
At the slightest sensation of touch.

I've looked and tried with many,
But all I could find was hurt,
And now I want you to know,
I want us to please make this work.

I've wondered if life was worth living,
And now I can say that's true,
For all of the things that you've done,
I want you to know I love you.
