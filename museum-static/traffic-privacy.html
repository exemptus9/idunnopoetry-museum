Privacy and audience statistics

The public museum, Lexical Observatory and project directory use anonymous statistics to understand which sections readers visit. The statistics count public pageviews, broad device type, approximate country, referring website names and outbound-link clicks. The museum uses a random identifier for a browser session, hashed separately for each project and day. Reports cover up to 180 days. Older records are removed in batches as visits are recorded or reports are refreshed; they may remain longer during inactivity.

Search and reclaim queries, usernames, private review tools, form contents, full referring URLs, names and raw IP addresses are not stored in analytics records. RhymeMosaic provides the statistics service; hosting and network providers may process ordinary technical request information. Counts can be incomplete and do not establish anonymity.
